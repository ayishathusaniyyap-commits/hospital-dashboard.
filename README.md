# 🏥 Hospital Operations & Patient Care Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Hospital Operations & Patient Care Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based dashboard designed to analyze hospital performance, patient care, staff workload, revenue, costs, and operational efficiency.

The dashboard transforms hospital operational data into meaningful KPIs and visual insights, helping users understand patient activity, departmental performance, overtime, waiting times, insurance performance, and patient satisfaction.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall hospital performance using key KPIs.
- Analyze patient visits and bed occupancy.
- Track average length of stay and patient wait times.
- Evaluate department-wise patient volume and performance.
- Analyze staff overtime and working hours.
- Compare hospital revenue and operational costs.
- Understand patient satisfaction across departments.
- Analyze insurance types based on length of stay and costs.
- Identify departments with higher waiting times and overtime.

---

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following major KPIs:

- 👥 **Total Patient Visits**
- 🛏️ **Total Beds Occupied**
- 🏠 **Average Length of Stay**
- ⏱️ **Average Wait Time**
- 🕐 **Total Overtime Hours**
- 💰 **Total Billed Amount**
- 💵 **Cost Per Visit**
- ⭐ **Average Patient Satisfaction Score**
- 📈 **Cost to Revenue Ratio**

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Monthly Patient Visits & Average Length of Stay
Analyzes patient visit trends throughout the year and compares them with the average length of stay.

### 2. Department-Wise Bed Occupancy & Patient Visits
Compares patient visits and occupied beds across different hospital departments.

### 3. Overtime Analysis
Provides insights into overtime hours based on:

- Day of the week
- Department
- Staff role

### 4. Department Revenue vs. Cost Analysis
Compares department-level revenue and operational costs to understand financial performance.

### 5. Patient Satisfaction & Department Performance
Analyzes patient satisfaction scores across different hospital departments.

### 6. Staff Hours vs. Wait Time
Examines the relationship between staff working hours and patient waiting times by department.

### 7. Insurance Type Analysis
Compares insurance categories based on:

- Average length of stay
- Billed amount
- Cost amount

### 8. Wait Time & Overtime by Department
Analyzes department-level waiting time alongside overtime hours to identify operational workload patterns.

- 📅 Month
- 🏥 Department
- 🚑 Admission Type
- 🛏️ Length of Stay (Days)

These filters allow users to dynamically analyze different areas of hospital operations.

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard helps users identify:

- Patient volume trends over time.
- Departments with high patient activity.
- Areas with higher overtime requirements.
- Differences between department revenue and cost.
- Patient satisfaction levels by department.
- Insurance categories associated with different lengths of stay.
- Departments experiencing higher waiting times.
- The relationship between staffing, overtime, and patient care.
